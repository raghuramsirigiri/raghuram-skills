# Larkmouth Ferries — annual summary for passengers, 2025

*Invented for the evals. Every name and figure below is fictitious.*

¶1 Larkmouth Ferries runs three routes across the Lark estuary. This summary
sets out how the year compared with 2024.

¶2 The adult single fare rose from £3.40 to £3.70. The concession fare was
held at £1.80.

¶3 Commuters made up 62% of journeys, up from 58%. Leisure travel was 31%
and school travel 7%.

¶4 In our passenger survey, 78% said they were satisfied or very satisfied
with the service, compared with 81% in 2024. The survey's margin of error
is about ±3 points, so we cannot say whether satisfaction really fell.

¶5 Operating costs per journey were an estimated £4.10, against £3.90 the
year before. Fares covered about 71% of costs; the county subsidy covered
the rest.

¶6 Fuel made up 38% of operating costs, up from 33%. We do not attribute the
fare rise to any single cost.

¶7 We expect the share of commuters could pass two-thirds by 2028 if current
trends continue.
