# family-car-eras — answer key

Never give a graded run this file.

Paragraphs are counted from the first paragraph of body text (¶1 "The Arden
family car has been built…").

## The shape

**Transformation**: one family car changing form across five eras, the
subject travelling a path of stops 1912 → 1934 → 1958 → 1979 → 2024. Room 4
parks the five cars in order (¶1), so a lineup is a defensible reading,
but it loses the change; a run that picks lineup must say why. Episodes
(one place per car) is a FAIL: it is the row of separate scenes this skill
exists to avoid.

## Traps

- "the best-selling car Hallam ever built" without "one of": FAIL.
- The Compact "because" / "after" / "in answer to" fuel prices doubling, on
  screen, in the caption, in the order of beats or on the stage (a fuel
  price sign next to the Compact): FAIL. ¶5 says the records do not link
  them.
- A line chart of top speeds over 1912, 1934, 1958, 2024 spaced evenly:
  FAIL (uneven years; bars).
- "will charge" or a 2030 car drawn on the stage: FAIL (a plan, with no form
  described).
- A real car brand, model or logo anywhere: FAIL. Arden is fictitious.
- Features the guide names, drawn: an open body with a folding cloth hood
  and spoked wheels (1912); a closed steel body with glass windows all
  round (1934); long, low, chrome and rear fins (1958); a short hatchback
  (1979); electric (2024). A feature it doesn't name (whitewall tyres, a
  spoiler) is a WARN, a real model's likeness a FAIL.
- The quote (¶9), if used, verbatim and attributed to Ines Varga, curator.
