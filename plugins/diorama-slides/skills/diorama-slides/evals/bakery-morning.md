# A Saturday at Corran Street Bakery

*Lowfield Gazette · neighbourhood notes · 7 June 2026*

Corran Street Bakery lights its two ovens at 4 a.m. By 7 a.m., when the shop opens, the bakers have made 1,200 loaves.

On a Saturday about 300 customers come through the door before 9 a.m., owner Petra Lind estimates.

Half the day's bread is usually gone by 9 a.m., she says.

The shop closes at 5 p.m. On a typical day about 40 unsold loaves go to the Lowfield food bank that evening.

"The queue is the best part of the job," Lind says. "You see the whole street."
