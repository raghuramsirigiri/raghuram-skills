# action-titles — evals

**For whoever runs the evals, not for the skill.** Nothing here is needed to use
`action-titles`, and a graded run must not read this file, any `.KEY.md`, or `evals.json` —
they describe what the fixtures contain, and a run that knows the answer isn't being tested.
Point the run at the fixture and at `SKILL.md` plus `references/`; nothing else under `evals/`.

## Fixtures

- `qbr-deck-q3-2026.pptx` — a 14-slide customer-operations QBR. Plants a topic label over a
  native chart, a title that is already right, a causal title the slide doesn't support (with
  the cause guessed in the notes), a picture-of-a-chart, two unrelated metrics on one slide, a
  verdict adjective on a 1-point move, bad news under a neutral label, a forecast, an org chart
  whose notes hold a figure that isn't on the slide, and an appendix. Graded by
  `qbr-deck-q3-2026.KEY.md` (evals 1, 4, 5, 6).
- `pasted-slides-sales.md` — four slides pasted as text: a coverage ratio below target, a
  causal title with no cause on the slide, a forecast, and a rate change that must be stated
  in points (eval 2).

The deck is generated by a private script; regenerate it rather than editing the `.pptx`.
All names, companies and figures are invented. None of the fixtures' figures appear in
`SKILL.md` or `references/` — the references' examples use their own invented numbers so they
teach the rules, not the answers.
