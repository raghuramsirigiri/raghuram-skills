## Passes

- 16:9, about 45 s, three evidence scenes, one per insight (or two plus a clearly caveated
  third).
- Delivery: 61% → 72% (before-after or compare-bars), "of members who used delivery" in the
  units or kicker.
- Recommend: 64% → 58%, a fall, stated plainly.
- App users: if shown, the small base is **on screen** in that scene ("46 app users",
  "small sample", "indicative"), and the caption says so too. Leaving insight 3 out and saying
  why also passes.
- Changes stated in points (+11 points, −6 points), never "up 11%" or "down 6%".
- No reason for any change: the notes say the "why" isn't analysed yet.
- Source card: Linden Co-op customer survey 2026, July 2026 fieldwork or 12 Aug 2026.

## Fails

- 65% presented as a finding about all app users or all members, or without its base.
- "Up 18%" for 61 → 72 (that is a relative change the notes don't state; points are the
  measure).
- Any cause ("thanks to the new app", "because of faster vans").
