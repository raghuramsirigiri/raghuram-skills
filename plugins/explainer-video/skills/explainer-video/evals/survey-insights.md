# Linden Co-op — customer survey 2026: key insights (internal notes)

Prepared by the insights team, 12 August 2026. Online survey of Linden Co-op
members, fieldwork 1–21 July 2026, 1,204 responses.

## Insight 1 — Delivery is now the strongest part of the service

- 72% of respondents rated home delivery "good" or "very good" (base: 868
  members who used delivery in the last three months).
- That compares with 61% in last year's survey (same question, base 790).

## Insight 2 — Members would recommend us, but less strongly than before

- 58% said they were likely or very likely to recommend Linden Co-op to a
  friend (base: all 1,204).
- In 2025 the figure was 64%.

## Insight 3 — App users lean towards click-and-collect

- Among members who mainly shop through the new app, 65% said they prefer
  click-and-collect to home delivery.
- **Caveat: small base (n = 46). Treat as indicative only; do not quote as a
  finding about all app users.**

## Notes

- Percentages are of those who answered each question.
- We have not analysed why satisfaction changed; open-text coding is due in
  September.
