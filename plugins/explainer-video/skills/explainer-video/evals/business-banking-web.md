# Fernhill Bank — Business Banking digital sales and website analytics, Q3 2026

*Internal note, Digital Sales team, 9 October 2026. Figures for July to September 2026
unless stated. Website figures cover the business product pages only (not online banking).*

## 1. Online share of new accounts

46% of new business current accounts in Q3 2026 were opened online, compared with 41% in
Q3 2025. The rest were opened in branch or by phone with a relationship manager.

## 2. From product page to opened account

The business product pages had 412,000 visits in the quarter. Roughly 1.5% of those visits
ended in an opened account.

Of the applications started online, 18,600 were started, 9,200 were submitted and 6,100
accounts were opened. That means about half of started applications (51%) were never
submitted.

## 3. Openings by quarter

Accounts opened online rose every quarter this year:

| Quarter | Accounts opened online |
| --- | --- |
| Q4 2025 | 4,200 |
| Q1 2026 | 4,800 |
| Q2 2026 | 5,500 |
| Q3 2026 | 6,100 |

## 4. Devices

58% of product page visits came from mobile; 42% from desktop and tablet.
