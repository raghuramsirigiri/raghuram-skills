# Metro Rides: city operations dashboard, Q3 2026

*Fictional company and figures, written as a test document.*

## Headline numbers

- Completed trips: 4.6 million in Q3, up 9% on Q2.
- Active drivers: 18,400 in an average week, down from 19,100 in Q2.
- Average pickup wait: 6.2 minutes across the quarter.
- Rider cancellations: 11% of requested trips were cancelled by the rider before pickup.

## When riders wait

Waits are short through the middle of the day and long at the edges of it.
Between 07:00 and 09:00 the average wait reaches 9.8 minutes, and on Friday
and Saturday nights between 23:00 and 02:00 it reaches 12.4 minutes. Midday
waits average 4.1 minutes.

The longer a rider waits, the more likely they are to give up: when the wait
shown at booking is over 10 minutes, 27% of riders cancel, compared with 6%
when it is under 5 minutes.

## Where the drivers are

Driver supply does not follow demand. At the morning peak, 38% of online
drivers are already on the airport rank, where queues of drivers wait for
flights, while the business district has the most requests. The airport
accounts for 14% of trips but holds more than a third of idle drivers at
peak.

## The rain week

In the week of 14 September, heavy rain on four days pushed requests up 31%
against a normal week. Wait times rose to an average of 11.0 minutes and
cancellations to 19%. Surge pricing brought more drivers online, but most of
them arrived after the evening peak had passed.

## What changes in Q4

Operations will trial a cap on drivers queueing at the airport at peak
times, and a bonus for drivers who log on in the business district before
07:00. The goal for Q4 is an average wait under 5 minutes at the morning
peak.
