# Screenplay: Walking first

Logline: The camera walks a single path through Africa, out across the continents and into the cold, meeting each of our ancestors where they lived, and finds that we stood up long before we grew big brains, and that our species is a late, well-travelled arrival.
Shape: a chronology along one path through time and place

## Act 1: Walking first

### B1 · 4 s · establishing
Place: N12
Cast: N5
Camera: high over an African woodland at dawn, drifting down
Action: Mist lifts off the trees; a path runs away through them.
Caption: Walking first: how we became human
Claims: —

### B2 · 5 s
Place: N12
Cast: N1, N5
Camera: push in to the edge of the trees
Action: A small figure steps out of the woodland and stands up.
Callout: about 7 million years ago
Caption: Sahelanthropus, in Chad, may be among the first of our line.
Claims: C1

### B3 · 5 s
Place: N12
Cast: N2, N3
Camera: low along a trail in grey ash, tracking two walkers
Action: Two small figures walk upright across the ash, leaving footprints.
Callout: 3.66 million years ago
Caption: Footprints at Laetoli: an upright stride.
Claims: C2

### B4 · 5 s
Place: N12
Cast: N4, N5
Camera: slow orbit around a small figure beside a tree
Action: Lucy stands upright, then reaches up into the branches.
Callout: 3.2 million years ago
Caption: Lucy: a little over 1 metre tall, upright, still climbing.
Claims: C3

## Act 4: Our species

### B10 · 5 s
Place: N12
Cast: N11

Callout: 300,000 years ago
Caption: The oldest known Homo sapiens, Jebel Irhoud, Morocco.

### B11 · 6 s
Place: N12
Cast: N4, N7, N10, N11, N16
Camera: tracking along four figures in a row, oldest to newest
Action: Beside each species a pile of pebbles builds up, one pebble per 50 cubic centimetres of brain.
Count: 27 pebbles beside Homo sapiens (1 pebble = 50 cubic centimetres)
Key: 1 pebble = 50 cubic centimetres of brain
Callout: 1,350 cubic centimetres
Caption: Brains grew from 400 to 500 in Lucy's species to about 1,350 in ours.
