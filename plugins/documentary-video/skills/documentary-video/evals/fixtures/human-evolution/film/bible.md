# Bible: Walking first

## Setting
Era: from about 7 million to about 15,000 years ago; East and North Africa, the Caucasus, ice-age Europe, then the world
World tone: neutral greys; Africa warm-light greys (n0a ground patches, n2a grass), Europe snow as `paper`; `hl` only for what a beat counts (pebbles, beads) and the species a beat is about when it stands among others
Not in this world: modern clothes or buildings, metal, wheels, domesticated animals, writing; no species labels on bodies; no faces beyond a brow and jaw; nothing implying who made the oldest tools

## Characters

### K1 · Sahelanthropus · N1
Story job: the very first figure, possibly of our line; seen once, from mid-distance
Tell apart from: Lucy (K3) and later humans: smaller, more stooped, more ape-like; never on screen with them
Look: the plain figure, short (1.2 m), a strong forward stoop, long arms, short legs, dark all over (n4), a heavy brow
States: standing up from a crouch
Scale: 1.2 m

### K2 · Laetoli walkers · N2
Story job: two small upright walkers making the footprints; who they were the document doesn't say
Tell apart from: nothing else is on the ash; plain small upright figures
Look: as K3's build but unnamed: 1.1-1.3 m, slight stoop, long arms, dark grey (n3)
States: walking
Scale: 1.1 m and 1.3 m

### K3 · Lucy, Australopithecus afarensis · N4
Story job: upright but small-brained; climbs as well as walks; first of the four in the brain row
Tell apart from: Homo erectus (K6): much shorter, longer arms, shorter legs, a slight stoop
Look: the plain figure at 1.1 m, arms long (0.42), legs short (0.42), slight stoop, n3 all over, a jutting jaw
States: standing, reaching up into a tree, standing in the row; lit
Scale: 1.1 m

### K6 · Homo erectus · N7
Story job: the modern body and the first to leave Africa; holds a hand axe
Tell apart from: Lucy: tall, long legs, upright; from Homo sapiens: heavier brow, sloping forehead
Look: the plain figure at 1.7 m with modern proportions, n2 all over, a heavy brow ridge, a hand axe in the right hand
States: walking (long strides), sitting by the fire, standing in the row; lit
Scale: 1.7 m

### K7 · Neanderthal · N10
Story job: the other humans, built for the cold, with brains as large as ours
Tell apart from: Homo sapiens: stockier and broader, shorter legs, a fur cape over the shoulders
Look: the plain figure at 1.65 m, wide shoulders and deep chest (depth 0.1, shoulders 0.14), short legs, a rounded fur cape (n4), heavy brow
States: walking in snow, standing in the row; lit
Scale: 1.65 m

### K8 · Homo sapiens · N11
Story job: our species: the last of the row, the walkers on the map, the bead wearer
Tell apart from: Neanderthals: slender, taller, a high rounded head, a light wrap
Look: the plain figure at 1.75 m, slim, a pale wrap (paper) round the waist and one shoulder, a spear on the long walks; a string of beads in close-up
States: standing, walking, standing in the row; lit
Scale: 1.75 m

## Props

### K4 · footprints in volcanic ash · N3
Story job: the proof of upright walking at Laetoli
Tell apart from: nothing; a trail of small dark prints in a grey strip of ash
Look: a long flat strip of ash (n2a), small dark oval prints (n4 flats) in two staggered lines, appearing as the walkers pass
States: appearing one by one
Scale: prints 0.2 m long; trail 20 m

### K5 · trees · N5
Story job: Lucy's tree; Africa's woodland and grassland; Europe's cold forest. The trees tell the viewer where they are
Tell apart from: each other by silhouette only: a round woodland tree (Africa), a flat-topped acacia (open grassland), a stacked conifer (ice-age Europe)
Look: faceted lumps on six-sided trunks; acacia: a wide flat facet canopy on a thin forked trunk; conifer: three to four stacked cones, snow on top
States: one look each
Scale: 6-10 m tall

### K9 · stone tools · N6
Story job: the oldest stone tools; a strike and a flake
Tell apart from: pebbles (K12): larger, angular, a fresh flake face
Look: a faceted core stone and a hammer stone (facet, detail 0), a thin flake (an extruded shard)
States: whole, struck (flake off)
Scale: 0.1-0.15 m

### K10 · hand axe · N8
Story job: Homo erectus' tool, carried in hand
Tell apart from: the stone tools: a symmetrical teardrop shape
Look: a flat teardrop (extruded outline), faceted edges
States: one
Scale: 0.15 m

### K11 · fire · N9
Story job: early fire, at night at a cave mouth
Tell apart from: nothing at night glows but it
Look: a ring of stones, a few sticks, three or four glowing flame cones (glow)
States: burning
Scale: 0.8 m across

### K12 · pebbles of brain size · N16
Story job: one pebble per 50 cubic centimetres: about 9 for Lucy's species, 20 for erectus, 30 for Neanderthals, 27 for us
Tell apart from: the stone tools: small, rounded, all the same
Look: small rounded pebbles (rounded, round 0.8) stacked into a low heap beside each figure
States: building up one by one; neutral, lit
Scale: 0.12 m each

### K13 · string of 100 shell beads · N18
Story job: 1 to 2 percent of a person's DNA: 1 bead lit, 1 lit softly
Tell apart from: nothing else is close up; a loop of small beads on a cord
Look: 100 small flattened beads (rounded) on a thin cord (tube), in a loop round the traveller's neck, seen close
States: all neutral; one lit (hl), one soft (hlSoft)
Scale: beads 0.012 m; loop 0.5 m long

## Places

### K14 · Africa · N12
Story job: the long path: woodland, the ash at Laetoli, open grassland, a cave mouth, a rocky rise in Morocco
Tell apart from: Europe: warm open ground, round trees and acacias, no snow
Look: ground patches (flat, n0a), grass tufts (small facet lumps), boulders and low hills (facet), a cave mouth (a big facet rock with a dark opening), all along one path
States: dawn, day, night
Scale: the path about 400 m long

### K15 · Europe in the cold · N13
Story job: the Neanderthals' world
Tell apart from: Africa: snow ground, conifers, cold light
Look: a snowfield (flat paper), conifers, grey boulders
States: day
Scale: a valley about 120 m across

### K16 · the raised map · N12 N14 N15
Story job: where the walkers cross continents: out of Africa to Dmanisi, then the spread to Australia and the Americas
Tell apart from: the landscapes: a low relief of continents on a flat sea, seen from above, with pinned place names
Look: rough continent outlines extruded 2 m high (n0a) on a sea plate (n1), at about 1:100,000; walking figures shown as small markers on the routes
States: routes drawing in
Scale: about 400 m across
