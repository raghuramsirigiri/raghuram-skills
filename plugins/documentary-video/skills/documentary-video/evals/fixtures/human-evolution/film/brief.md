# Brief: Walking first

Thesis: Our ancestors walked upright millions of years before their brains grew, and our own species is young: it left Africa only about 60,000 to 70,000 years ago and still carries traces of the Neanderthals it met.
Audience: curious general viewers; no background in anthropology assumed.
Length: 70 s
Frame: 16:9
Document: human-evolution.md (a test document written from commonly cited figures; dates approximate)
