# Bible: Waiting at the kerb

## Setting
Era: 2026, a present-day mid-sized city with a business district, residential streets and an airport on its edge
World tone: neutral greys throughout; `hl` for whatever the beat counts (the cars on the rank, the riders who walk away); light changes with the time of day
Not in this world: real ride-hailing brands, logos or app screens; taxis with roof signs (these are private cars); real city landmarks

## Characters

### K1 · rider · N1
Story job: the people waiting; a line of 100 at the kerb carries every share of riders (C8, C4, C12)
Tier: background (the crowd at the kerb); the rider B3 follows and the one holding the phone in B7 are hero
Tell apart from: drivers, who are only ever seen inside cars, so riders need nothing special; varied plain figures, some holding a phone
Look: plain rounded figures with no hands or feet, varied heights and greys, a few with a bag or a hood up in the rain
States: standing in line, stepping out and walking away, getting into a car; lit
Scale: 1.55-1.9 m

### K2 · driver · N2
Story job: always shown as their car; the driver is the head and shoulders seen through the windows
Tier: background (a head behind a windscreen)
Tell apart from: riders: drivers sit, riders stand
Look: a seated figure's head and shoulders, cut at the car's waist
States: seated
Scale: 1.7 m figure, seated

## Props

### K3 · ride-hailing car · N3
Story job: each car is one driver online or one trip; 100 of them on the rank and on the streets carry C9 and C10
Tier: supporting (the rank and the streets); the car that pulls up in B3 is hero
Tell apart from: nothing else on the streets moves, so a generic small hatchback is enough; no roof sign
Look: a rounded body with a darker glasshouse band and four dark wheels, bevelled and simple; all cars identical
States: parked, driving, pulling away with a rider; lit
Scale: 4.1 x 1.8 x 1.5 m

### K4 · rider's phone · N4
Story job: shows the wait at booking in B6 and B7, close up
Tier: hero (B7 opens on it in close-up)
Tell apart from: it only appears close up, so a dark slab with a pale screen and one bar of wait time reads
Look: a thin bevelled slab, screen as a decal: a short bar (under 5 minutes) or a long bar (over 10)
States: short wait shown, long wait shown
Scale: 0.15 x 0.075 x 0.01 m; seen in a rider's hand

## Places

### K5 · airport rank · N5
Story job: where 38% of drivers wait at the morning peak (B4) while only 14% of trips start there (B5)
Tier: supporting (the camera arrives over the rank at about 100 m)
Tell apart from: the rest of the city: a low terminal with a long canopy and a wide car park marked in bays
Look: one long low terminal block with a flat canopy, a control tower as a simple cylinder and cap, a large flat car park with painted bays (flat shapes), rows of parked cars
States: morning peak (rows full), Q4 (fewer cars)
Scale: terminal about 120 x 30 x 12 m; rank about 80 x 40 m

### K6 · business district · N6
Story job: where the riders are at the morning peak, and where the cars go in Q4
Tier: supporting (towers at mid distance); the rest of the city is background
Tell apart from: residential streets: taller office blocks close together, with window-band decals
Look: a cluster of bevelled towers of different heights, flat roofs with a few roof boxes, kerbs along the streets between them
States: morning (long lines, empty kerbs), Q4 (cars arriving, lines shorter)
Scale: towers 20-60 m tall on blocks of about 40 x 40 m

### K7 · pickup point at the kerb · N7
Story job: the stage for every wait: a line of riders, a car pulling up
Tier: supporting (the kerb at 15-25 m)
Tell apart from: nothing; any kerb in the city
Look: a strip of pavement raised a little above the road, a street lamp, a low building front behind it
States: day, night (lamp lit), rain
Scale: pavement 3 m wide, 30 m of kerb in shot; lamp 5 m

### K8 · the city · N8
Story job: the wide shots that tie the places together: dawn, midday, night, rain
Tier: background (seen from far above)
Tell apart from: the places within it, which the camera lands on; the rest is plain
Look: a grid of streets between low bevelled blocks, faceted trees in a park, the business district in the middle and the airport at one edge
States: dawn, midday, night, rain
Scale: about 2 x 2 km, mostly seen from far above
