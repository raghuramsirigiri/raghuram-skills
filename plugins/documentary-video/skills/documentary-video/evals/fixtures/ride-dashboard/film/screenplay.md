# Screenplay: Waiting at the kerb

Logline: A day in Metro Rides' city, following riders at the kerb and the cars that should reach them, finds the drivers parked at the airport while the business district waits, and riders walking away as the wait grows.
Shape: a day in the life of the system, then the rain week, then the fix

Place: N8
Cast: N3
Camera: high wide shot over the city at dawn, slow orbit as cars move along the streets
Action: Cars stream through the streets, pulling away from kerbs with riders.
Caption: In Q3, Metro Rides completed 4.6 million trips, up 9%.
