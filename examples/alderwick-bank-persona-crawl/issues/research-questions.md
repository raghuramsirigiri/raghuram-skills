# Questions to test with real users

- Ask small-business owners on the merchant overview what they would pay per month on $15,000 of card sales and whether they expect any monthly fee; see whether anyone mentions PCI or the 9 p.m. cutoff.
- Watch bank switchers start the checking application without their old bank's details: does a step-1 note that funding can wait 30 days prevent early exits like walk 1's?
- Ask CD shoppers on phones, after using /rates, what it costs to withdraw early; check whether a penalty line next to the CD table is noticed.
- Watch low-vision users at 150-200% zoom complete the wealth form and note whether the consent checkbox is missed before submit.
