What changed

Rides rose 22%, and the gap opened in April

The two seasons ran level through March. From the month the e-bikes arrived, every month of 2025 beat the same month of 2024 by a fifth or more, and the lead held into the winter.

  • 2.85m rides in 2025, up from 2.34m
  • +79k rides in July alone, the widest gap
  • No fare change and no new stations in the year

Saturday afternoons are now the busiest hours of the week

Read. Weekday commutes still make two sharp peaks, but the broad weekend afternoon carries more rides per hour than either.

What it earned

E-bike fees brought in $0.9m of the $2.2m gain

Revenue rose from $7.9m to $10.1m. More rides paid for most of it, and the per-minute e-bike fee for nearly all of the rest.

  • Ride volume +$1.4m
  • E-bike fees +$0.9m
  • Memberships +$0.3m
  • Promotions −$0.4m, the spring launch offer
