Mobility analysis · Lakeshore Bike Share

E-bikes added 925,000 rides in their first season

They took 437,000 rides from the classic fleet along the way, so the system grew by about half of what the e-bike count alone suggests.

Planning & Insights · Lakeshore Transit Authority · Published Feb 2026
Season Apr — Dec 2025

01Most of the growth rode in on the new fleet

E-bikes reached the streets in April, and within four months they carried two of every five rides. Classic rides fell over the same months, but by less than e-bikes added, and the net gain held through the autumn.

02The new trips are longer and start on the hills

An e-bike trip from Bluff Road runs twice as far as a classic one from the same stations. In the flat districts by the water the difference is small, because the classic bike was never the obstacle there.

Recommendation Put the next 300 e-bikes on the hill districts first. Bluff Road, Old Mill and North Campus show the longest new trips, so each new bike there carries the most distance the classic fleet could not.
Sources. Trip records, Apr–Dec 2024 and 2025. Method. Medians of trips over one minute; distances are straight lines between docks, so they understate the real route by a similar share for both fleets. Note. Every figure is illustrative sample data for a fictional city.
